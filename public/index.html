HOW TO PLAY

  • Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the mobile joystick.
  • Collect glowing crystals to grow and score energy.
  • Head-to-head collisions kill both snakes.
  • Head-to-body and head-to-tail collisions kill the attacker.
  • Avoid your own body and the arena walls.
  • Survive until the timer hits zero to win the match.
